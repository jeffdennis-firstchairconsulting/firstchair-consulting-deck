# Halvard Freight — operator interview notes (Aug 2026)

## Scope
Interviews with six depot managers and the regional operations manager, 11–14 Aug 2026. All figures fictional.

## What managers said
- Ridgeway lost its anchor customer when the Carver contract ended in 2024; volume fell 22% and has not recovered.
- Easton expanded in 2023, adding dock capacity 41 miles from Brookline.
- Brookline sits 41 miles from Easton. The Brookline manager estimates Tuesday volume could run out of Easton with no service change.
- Oakmont lease renews in March 2027 with a 12% rent step-up.
- Easton runs at about 71% dock utilisation and could absorb more volume with a second afternoon shift.
- Regional ops manager: "We could run Brookline whole Tuesday out of Easton afternoon shift and nobody would notice except the landlord."

## Constraints raised
- Ridgeway has a recognised union; consultation is required before any closure and typically takes four months.
- 14 of the 23 staff at the three sites live within 30 miles of a receiving site.
