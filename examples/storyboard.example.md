# Six depots do the work of nine

**Halvard Freight** · findings and recommendation · findings deck

**Audience.** Board of Halvard Freight: the CEO and CFO decide; two non-executive directors will push back on service risk; low technical depth

**Objective.** Approve the consolidation of three depots and release a $2.6M transition budget

**Grounded in.** Built on the FY25 depot P&L, the route model v3 and operator interviews from Aug 2026. All figures fictional.

## Setup

**Situation.** Halvard runs nine depots built for the 2019 volume mix.

**Complication.** Three of them have run below 40% utilisation for six quarters, and Oakmont's lease renews in March at a 12% step-up.

**Question.** Can the network lose three depots without losing service?

## The argument

> **Consolidating three depots saves $4.1M a year for a one-time cost of $2.6M, with next-day coverage held for 99.2% of customers.**

*Structure: inductive, grouped by why — 3 key lines.*

### 1. Three depots carry $5.2M of fixed cost for 14% of network volume.

- Ridgeway, Brookline and Oakmont run at 34–39% utilisation — FY25 dock hours used over available *(stated)*
- The three have been below 45% for six quarters — Quarterly utilisation Q1 25 to Q2 26 *(stated)*
- Their fixed cost is $5.2M against 14% of volume — Lease + labour from the P&L; volume share from the route model *(derived)*

  - slide → `heatmap` (trend) · **Three depots have run below 45% utilisation for six quarters.**
  - slide → `leading_to` (convergence) · **Three separate changes emptied the same three depots.**

### 2. Their volume fits inside Easton and Fairmont with 18% headroom, and next-day coverage holds for 99.2% of customers.

- Easton at 71% and Fairmont at 66% have spare dock capacity — FY25 utilisation *(stated)*
- Re-routed peak week models at 91% — Route model v3 peak run *(stated)*
- 17 of 2,140 customers lose next-day service — Route model v3 drive-time windows *(stated)*

  - slide → `scorecard` (comparison) · **Easton and Fairmont score highest on every criterion but one.**
  - slide → `funnel` (convergence) · **Of 2,140 customers, 17 would lose next-day service.**

### 3. The saving is $4.1M a year net, for a one-time cost of $2.6M, with payback inside eight months.

- Lease $2.3M, labour $1.6M, overhead $0.5M, less fuel $0.3M — Transition model v3 *(stated)*
- One-time cost $2.6M including lease exit penalties — Transition model v3 *(stated)*
- Payback in eight months — 2.6 / (4.1/12) = 7.6 months *(derived)*

  - slide → `waterfall` (bridge) · **Gross saving of $4.4M nets to $4.1M after fuel.**
  - slide → `roadmap` (plan) · **Savings start in month four and reach run-rate in month ten.**

### Risks

- **Union consultation at Ridgeway extends beyond four months** (High) — owner HR director; Start informal engagement now; sequence Oakmont and Brookline first so savings begin regardless.
- **Easton peak-week capacity is tighter than modelled** (Medium) — owner Ops director; Pilot a peak week in December before committing; Fairmont takes overflow.
- **Brookline sublet market softens** (Low) — owner Property; Exit penalty is already in the $2.6M; sublet is upside only.

### The decision

**Three approvals to start in November.**

- **Decision: Approve the plan** — Close Ridgeway, Brookline and Oakmont; Easton and Fairmont receive.
- **Budget: $2.6M transition** — Released in three tranches, one per phase.
- **Mandate: Start union engagement** — Informal engagement at Ridgeway from Nov 1.
